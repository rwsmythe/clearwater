## Why

The owner shoots an Insta360 camera on SCUBA dives and edits the recordings in Insta360 Studio, which exports flat 4K clips in two codecs. Clearwater's input rules admit both on paper (`.mov` by spec 5.2 since v0.5.7; `_PIX_FMT` in `io/video/probe.py:66` admits `yuv422p10le`; `yuvj` is read as full range at `:281-287`), but no package has run a ProRes 4:2:2 10-bit source or a full-range HEVC source through the real pipeline: every video figure of record is GoPro HEVC / H.264 4:2:0 limited range or iPhone `.MOV`. The chroma-upsampling path (spec: "chroma upsampling to full resolution before processing") has only seen 4:2:0. The exports are also an order of magnitude larger than the usage profile's clips. The owner needs to know what works and what it costs before relying on it.

## The design (CHARC's pass; the cell builds this unless round 0 disputes it with evidence)

1. **Inventory refresh:** `uv run python tools/inventory.py --out work/wp7.1/inventory` over the whole footage folder (no exclusion); the report in the 2026-10-29 form with a new section "11. Insta360"; the Phone and GoPro sections say "unchanged, see 2026-10-29" unless a count differs (then the difference is stated).
2. **Probe table:** for each of the seven files, the product's own probe result (accepted / refused, the colour tags as read, the range as read, the pixel format, the decode path chosen) from the real code path (the headless export's probe step or the probe module directly, under the Entry-point isolation rule: redirected roots, no GUI).
3. **Decode agreement:** for the 62 s video, decode frame 0 and the frame at 30 s from BOTH sources through the product's decode path to the pipeline's working format; report per-channel mean absolute difference and the 99th percentile; the expected band is the HEVC quantisation plus the 4:2:2 -> 4:2:0 difference (small); a difference consistent with a range double-expansion or a missing chroma upsample is a STOP (a product defect). Repeat frame 0 only for the 439 s video.
4. **Exports:** `SCUBA vids 2.mov` and `SCUBA vids2.mp4` corrected end to end with the default preset (c3) through the headless export (the spawned worker, as the GUI does), outputs under `work/wp7.1/out/`; V4 on each; `SCUBA vids 3.mp4` the same (439 s). The ProRes export is the one that exercises the decode cost; both outputs are compared with the WP3.21-style oracle comparison (the two corrected outputs of the same video from the two sources should agree within the decode band of item 3 plus the encoder's).
5. **Throughput:** EXCLUSIVE; the whole 62 s clip and the whole 439 s clip from each source, wall time and the per-stage split `bench.py` records (decode, correct, encode), on the GPU path; the ProRes decode rate alone (FFmpeg decode to the pipe, no correction) as the isolating figure; against the N6 figure of record (10.89 min/min cold at 4K). Memory peak per the V16 form.
6. **The long clip:** the projection from item 5's per-minute figures for 37.7 min from each source (wall, output size at the default bitrate, peak memory, disk under `work/`); the run itself only on the owner's word, overnight, EXCLUSIVE, with the journal's leftover behaviour noted if it is interrupted.
7. **The look pack:** `work/wp7.1/look/index.html`, before / after at 10 s, 30 s, 50 s of the 62 s video from both sources side by side, and three timestamps of the 439 s one; the owner's look is owner-reserved and recorded by CHARC.
