## Why

WP6.3b pinned BtbN's `win64-gpl-9.0` autobuild (`autobuild-2026-10-31-12-59`, build-scripts commit `5031c6f1`, FFmpeg `n9.0.2-39-g7adfbe40dc`). Its seat licence pass (`wp6.3b/seat/licences-5031c6f1.md` section 3) found that the binary is GPL-3.0 by `--enable-version3` and statically links zvbi `34bb6f66`, whose `src/packet-830.*` and `src/pdc.*` are GPL-2.0-only, compiled unconditionally and reached from the teletext decoder FFmpeg links. GPL-2.0-only code cannot be combined into a GPL-3.0 program, so the asset as published is not cleanly redistributable. The other 102 component slots verified compatible. Clearwater uses no teletext or caption decoding. The owner ruled self-build (2026-11-05: "self-build is fine"). The minimal correct change is BtbN's own scripts at the same commit with the zvbi stage disabled, which keeps the component provenance WP6.3b already tabulated (`wp6.3b/seat/scriptsd-5031c6f1.md`), keeps D26's encoder set, and, as a side benefit, puts the asset under the owner's account where BtbN's two-year retention policy no longer applies.
